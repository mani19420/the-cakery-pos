Dual Tax System

Tax Settings

Pakistan tax setup: FBR (Federal Board of Revenue) applies to your main menu items (food, bakery, desserts). PRA (Punjab Revenue Authority) applies to beverages only — coffee, matcha, shakes, juices, tea. Both rates are saved here and used on every customer order and POS bill.

FBR — Main Menu (Food/Bakery)

Default 5% — applies to breads, viennoiserie, patisserie, cakes, slices, chef's whisk, sandwiches, add-ons.

PRA Punjab — Beverages

PRA rate now depends on the customer's payment method: 16% on cash, 8% on card / online (Punjab restaurant services). The cart total recomputes automatically when the customer switches between Cash and Card at checkout.

Beverage Categories

Tick which categories count as "beverages" — PRA applies to items in these categories only. Everything else gets FBR.

Price Display

If unchecked (default), prices are tax-exclusive — taxes are added on top at checkout.

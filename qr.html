How to set up your tables

  1. Enter the number of tables in your café above.
  2. Each card below shows a unique QR code for one table.
  3. Click the Download icon to save the QR as a PNG image.
  4. Print each QR and place it on the matching table.
  5. When a customer scans the QR, they'll see the menu with their table number auto-selected.
  6. When they place an order, it pops up on the POS dashboard instantly.
